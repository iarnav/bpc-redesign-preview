About BPC

Unity in Diversity

A community of Indians in Dorset celebrating culture, organising events and supporting each other.

Who we are

The heart of our Indian community

BPC is more than just a group — it's a family brought together by shared traditions, vibrant celebrations and a spirit of togetherness.

From cultural festivals and wellness programmes to charity drives and social events, we create moments that connect hearts and celebrate our rich heritage. We welcome the Indian community and friends of every background across Bournemouth, Poole and Christchurch — preserving traditions, embracing diversity and building a stronger community for everyone.

Our story

From a radio show to a community

BPC began with music. What started as Music Lovers of Bollywood — a small group and a weekly Bollywood music broadcast on local radio — grew into a thriving network of professionals, families and friends. Today the BPC Indian Community offers social, cultural and educational activities all year round, from Holi and Diwali to dance, wellbeing, networking and charity events.

Our journey

How we got here

  1. 2018

    Music Lovers Bollywood begins

    A weekly Bollywood music broadcast and small gatherings grow into bigger and bigger events, with Bollywood Beats becoming Dorset's first Bollywood radio show.

  2. 2019

    BPC Indian Community is founded

    The Bournemouth-Poole-Christchurch Indian Community is set up to take the cultural mission further, for families and friends of every background.

  3. 2020

    Standing together through COVID-19

    The community comes together for COVID-19 relief efforts and charity events, supporting people when it mattered most.

  4. 2022

    Women of Excellence

    Our International Women's Award celebrates the strength, resilience and achievements of women in our community.

  5. 2026

    Diwali at Corfe Castle

    A celebration of light, culture and community in collaboration with the National Trust.

Our logo

The colours we carry

Our logo carries India's tricolour, its saffron and green drifting like colour thrown at Holi, around the Ashoka Chakra, with Bournemouth at its heart. It's a reminder of the culture we share, celebrated here in Bournemouth, Poole and Christchurch.

  • Saffron courage
  • White peace
  • Green growth
  • Chakra progress

What drives us

Vision & mission

Our vision

To build a lively, inclusive community that honours Indian culture, strengthens connections and enhances lives through music, social activities and cultural preservation.

Our mission

To bring people together across Bournemouth, Poole and Christchurch through music, wellness programmes and cultural events, encouraging diversity, strength and mutual support.

Our three pillars

🪔

Cultural celebration

Honouring Indian traditions through festivals like Diwali and Holi, and strengthening the bonds between us.

💚

Support & wellness

Looking out for each other, from healthcare initiatives to mental wellbeing resources.

🤝

Networking & collaboration

Partnering with local organisations to strengthen ties and make a positive impact on the wider community.
